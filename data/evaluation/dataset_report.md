## Known gaps and cautions

- No RSU award agreement (real) is available; only the synthetic RSU grant exists.
- Source URLs were recorded only where recoverable from the files (one SEC filing). Other download URLs and all Kaggle/source identifiers and licenses are unknown and must be confirmed before redistribution.
- The personal-finance dataset is synthetic, individual-level and has no monthly history, assets, holdings or obligations beyond one loan; it must not be presented as real client data.
- Three real contracts are browser prints of SEC HTML; their page numbers are print pages.
- The UBS credit line agreement states no numeric collateral maintenance level or advance rate.
- The Parkway LPA is a real-estate office fund agreement (2008), not a private-equity buyout LPA.
